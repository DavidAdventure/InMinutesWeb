InMinutes Privacy Policy Oct 29, 2026 · Effective date: Oct 29, 2026 InMinutes delivers food, groceries and essentials from local stores to your door. To do that, we need some information about you, such as your name, phone number and delivery address. We use it to run the service, share it only with the people who need it to complete your order, and never sell it. Who we are InMinutes is operated by INMINUTES E-COMMERCE NIGERIA LIMITED (“InMinutes”, “we”, “us”), 3, Faisal Badmus, Havana Estate, Arepo, Ogun State, Nigeria. We are responsible for the personal information described in this policy. It covers the InMinutes mobile apps, our website at inminutesapp.com, and related services. Information we collect Information you give us • Account details: your name, phone number, email address and password. • Delivery details: addresses, landmarks and delivery instructions you save or enter. • Orders: what you order, from which store, special requests, ratings and reviews. • Support: messages you send us and details of any complaints or refunds. Information collected when you use InMinutes • Location: your device’s location while you use the app, if you allow it, so we can suggest nearby stores, set your delivery point and estimate delivery times. You can turn this off in your device settings and enter your address manually. • Device and usage information: device type, operating system, app version, IP address, crash reports and how you use features in the app. • Notifications: a device token so we can send order updates if you allow notifications. Payment information Payments are processed by Paystack. Your full card details go directly to Paystack and are not stored by InMinutes. We receive limited information such as payment status, amount, card type and the last four digits. Store partners and riders (Paddies) If you join InMinutes as a store partner or rider, we also collect what we need to work with you: business or identity details, contact details, payout account details and, for riders, location while on duty so orders can be assigned and tracked. How we use your information • To create and manage your account. • To take, prepare, deliver and track your orders. • To process payments, refunds and payouts. • To send order updates and respond to your questions. • To keep InMinutes safe, including preventing fraud and misuse. • To understand how the app is used and improve it. • To send offers and news, only where you have agreed. You can opt out at any time. • To meet our legal obligations. Under the Nigeria Data Protection Act 2023, we process your information because it is needed to provide the service you asked for, because you have consented (for example, to location or marketing), because we have a legitimate interest in running and protecting our service, or because the law requires it. Who we share it with • Stores you order from: your name, order details and, where needed, your phone number. • Riders (Paddies) delivering your order: your name, phone number, delivery address and instructions. • Service providers who help us run InMinutes, such as Paystack for payments, cloud hosting, maps, messaging and crash reporting. They may use your information only to provide their service to us. • Authorities, when the law requires it or to protect the safety of our users, riders or the public. • A buyer or successor, if InMinutes is merged, acquired or reorganised, under the same protections. We do not sell your personal information. How long we keep it We keep your information for as long as your account is active and as needed to provide the service. After you delete your account, we delete or anonymise your information, except records we must keep, such as transaction records, to meet legal, tax or accounting obligations. Your rights You have the right to: • Ask for a copy of the personal information we hold about you. • Correct information that is wrong or incomplete. • Ask us to delete your information. • Withdraw consent you have given, such as for location or marketing. • Object to, or ask us to restrict, certain uses of your information. • Ask for your information in a format you can move to another service. To use these rights, email hello@inminutesapp.com. If you are unhappy with how we handle your information, you can complain to the Nigeria Data Protection Commission. Deleting your account You can delete your account in the app under Profile, then Settings, then Delete account. You can also email hello@inminutesapp.com from the email address linked to your account. We will confirm when your account has been deleted. How we protect it We use reasonable technical and organisational measures to protect your information, including encrypted connections, access controls and limiting access to staff who need it. No system is completely secure, so keep your password private and tell us if you think someone has accessed your account. Transfers outside Nigeria Some of our service providers may store or process information outside Nigeria. When this happens, we take steps to protect your information as Nigerian data protection law requires. Children InMinutes is not intended for anyone under 18, and we do not knowingly collect information from children. If you believe a child has given us personal information, contact us and we will delete it. Changes to this policy We may update this policy from time to time. When we make important changes, we will tell you in the app or by email and update the effective date above.
