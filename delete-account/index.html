Delete your InMinutes account

This page explains how to delete your account in the InMinutes app, provided by INMINUTES E-COMMERCE NIGERIA LIMITED, and what happens to your data.

Delete it in the app

  1. Open the InMinutes app and sign in.
  2. Go to Profile, then Settings.
  3. Tap Delete account and confirm.

Or ask us to delete it

Can’t open the app? Send us a request and we’ll delete your account for you.

These buttons open your email app or WhatsApp with your request ready to send. To protect your account, we may ask you to confirm the request from the phone number or email linked to it.

What happens next

  • We confirm your request and delete your account within 30 days.
  • Any active order should be completed or cancelled first.
  • Once deleted, your account can’t be restored. You’re welcome to sign up again anytime.

What we delete and what we keep

  • Deleted: your name, phone number, email, saved addresses, order history linked to you, and app preferences.
  • Kept: transaction and payment records we must keep by law for tax and accounting, held securely and only as long as required.

For more detail, read our Privacy Policy or email hello@inminutesapp.com.
