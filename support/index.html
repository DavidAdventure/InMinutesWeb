Message us on WhatsApp with your order number and we'll check with your rider and the store straight away.

Send us your order number or payment reference by WhatsApp or email and we'll confirm the payment and arrange a refund if needed.

Use the password reset option on the login screen. If that doesn't work, contact us from the phone number on your account.
