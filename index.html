Policies

How refunds, your data and our service work.

Refund and cancellation policy

Cancelling an order

You can cancel for a full refund before a rider picks up your order. Once a store has started preparing or packing your order, we may deduct the cost of items already prepared.

When you get a refund

  • Items that are missing from your order.
  • Items that arrive damaged, spoiled or different from what you ordered.
  • Orders we cannot deliver.

How to request one

Contact us within 24 hours of delivery with your order number and a photo of the problem. We review every request within 2 working days.

How refunds are paid

Approved refunds go back to your original payment method and usually arrive within 5 to 10 working days, depending on your bank. You can also choose InMinutes credit, which is available immediately.

Things we can't refund

Perishable items that were delivered correctly and in good condition, or orders where the address or phone number provided was wrong and the rider could not reach you.
